Yebo Bookings

A WhatsApp and email booking assistant for restaurants, pubs and clubs. It answers enquiries in seconds, sends quotes, takes bookings and reminds guests. This demo uses a sample venue: tap the buttons like a customer would. All names, prices, photos and bank details are examples.
